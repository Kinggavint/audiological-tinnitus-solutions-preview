Why We Are Different

We Specialize in Tinnitus.

Tinnitus is not an additional service we offer. It is our specialty. It is at the center of everything we do.

When tinnitus is disrupting your sleep, concentration, work, relationships, or peace of mind, you do not simply want someone who treats tinnitus occasionally.

You want someone who understands tinnitus.

More Than a Sound

Tinnitus Is More Than a Sound.

Tinnitus can affect far more than what you hear. It can interfere with sleep, concentration, communication, work, relationships, emotional well being, and overall quality of life.

We believe treatment should focus on more than the sound. It should focus on giving you back your quality of life.

Our comprehensive, multidimensional evaluation looks beyond the traditional audiogram to assess your tinnitus, hearing and auditory function, sound tolerance, communication challenges, and the functional impact tinnitus has on your everyday life.

We use those findings to create a personalized treatment strategy designed around you, not around a product or predetermined therapy.

Understand First. Treat Second.

Understanding Guides Your Treatment.

Comprehensive Evaluation, then Individual Tinnitus Profile, then Personalized Treatment, then Measurable Progress.

We do not begin by deciding which tinnitus treatment to sell you. We begin by understanding you.

A Complete Answer

There Is More to Tinnitus Care Than Learning to Live With It.

Many people with tinnitus have been told: There is nothing you can do. You will have to get used to it. Your hearing is normal, so everything is fine.

We believe you deserve a more complete answer.

While there is currently no single treatment that eliminates every form of tinnitus for every person, that does not mean nothing can be done.

Modern tinnitus care focuses on reducing the impact tinnitus has on your life. With appropriate evaluation, education, treatment, and ongoing support, many people can experience meaningful improvements in tinnitus related distress, sleep, concentration, communication, and quality of life.

The goal is not simply to live with tinnitus. The goal is to live your life without tinnitus running it.
