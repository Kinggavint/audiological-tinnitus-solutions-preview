FDA-Cleared Bimodal Neuromodulation

Lenire: a new approach for chronic subjective tinnitus.

Lenire pairs gentle tongue stimulation with personalized sound to retrain how the brain processes tinnitus. In a major clinical study, the majority of patients reported meaningful improvement after a 12-week treatment course.

  • FDA cleared for chronic subjective tinnitus in adults
  • Backed by large-scale clinical trials
  • Delivered by a designated treatment office
  • Treatment course is 12 weeks of in-home use, with regular clinic visits
  • Eligibility is determined by an in-office assessment
