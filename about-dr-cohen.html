Meet Dr. Shahrzad Cohen

Dr. Shahrzad Cohen, Au.D.

Dedicated to Changing the Way Tinnitus Is Treated.

Board Certified Audiologist Certificate Holder in Tinnitus Management Certified Brain Injury Specialist

As a Board Certified Audiologist, Certified Brain Injury Specialist, and Certificate Holder in Tinnitus Management, Dr. Shahrzad Cohen has dedicated much of her career to evaluating and treating individuals living with tinnitus, hearing loss, sound sensitivity, auditory processing difficulties, and complex auditory conditions.

Her philosophy is simple: Treat the person, not just the tinnitus.

Dr. Cohen combines the science of audiology and tinnitus treatment with a deeply personalized approach to care. She looks beyond the sound itself to understand how tinnitus is affecting the individual, physically, functionally, and emotionally, and uses that understanding to guide treatment.

Her goal is not simply to help patients manage a sound. It is to help them regain control of their lives.
