For Medical Professionals

A Specialty Resource for Tinnitus Referrals.

Tinnitus is one of the most reported symptoms in medical, audiological, and behavioral health practice.

It is also one of the most under evaluated. Because tinnitus involves auditory processing, central nervous system activity, sleep, mood, cognition, attention, and quality of life, thorough evaluation and appropriate treatment often benefit from a dedicated, tinnitus focused clinical approach.

The Tinnitus Specialty Clinic offers comprehensive tinnitus evaluation and evidence based treatment for referred patients, and works collaboratively with referring healthcare professionals to support coordinated care.

What We Provide

What We Provide

  • Comprehensive Tinnitus EvaluationA multidimensional assessment including tinnitus history, audiological findings, sound tolerance testing, tinnitus psychoacoustics, and quality of life impact.
  • Advanced Audiological TestingStandard and extended audiological assessment when clinically appropriate, including cochlear function measurements and additional testing as indicated.
  • Auditory Processing AssessmentEvaluation of how the brain processes complex auditory information, particularly relevant for patients with tinnitus, listening difficulties, or post concussive or post trauma auditory symptoms.
  • Sound Sensitivity and Hyperacusis EvaluationAssessment and treatment planning for decreased sound tolerance that frequently coexists with tinnitus.
  • TBI and Blast Exposure Related Auditory SymptomsSpecialized evaluation for auditory symptoms following head injury, blast exposure, or other neurologic events.
  • Advanced Tinnitus TreatmentPersonalized treatment planning that may include evidence based tinnitus management, sound therapy, advanced treatment technologies, and coordination with additional providers as appropriate.
  • Interdisciplinary CoordinationCommunication and coordination with ENT physicians, neurologists, primary care providers, psychologists, sleep specialists, physical therapists, dentists, and other healthcare professionals when indicated.
When To Refer

When to Refer

Consider referral to the Tinnitus Specialty Clinic when patients present with:

  • New, worsening, or persistent tinnitus, particularly when associated with impact on sleep, concentration, communication, mood, or daily functioning.
  • Tinnitus with normal hearing thresholds when patients continue to report bothersome symptoms.
  • Tinnitus associated with hearing loss, sound sensitivity, or hyperacusis.
  • Auditory symptoms following head injury, blast exposure, or neurologic events.
  • Patients with tinnitus who have been told nothing can be done and would benefit from a comprehensive tinnitus specialty evaluation.
  • Patients requesting information on advanced tinnitus treatment technologies including Lenire, Levo, Neuromonics, and emerging systems.

Certain tinnitus presentations require prompt medical evaluation prior to audiological referral, including pulsatile tinnitus, sudden hearing loss, associated neurologic symptoms, acute head or noise trauma, or severe emotional distress.

Referral Process

Referral Process

01

Patient Referral

Referrals may be initiated by phone, email, fax, or through your typical referral workflow. Patients may also self refer following your clinical recommendation.

02

Coordination of Records

When available, we appreciate access to prior audiological testing, medical history, imaging, or specialty consultation reports related to the tinnitus presentation.

03

Comprehensive Evaluation

Patients receive a specialty focused tinnitus evaluation designed to characterize the tinnitus, assess relevant auditory function, identify contributing factors, and develop a personalized treatment strategy.

04

Communication Back to the Referring Provider

Following evaluation, referring providers receive a summary of findings, recommendations, and proposed treatment plan, as clinically appropriate and with patient consent.

05

Ongoing Coordination

We maintain open lines of communication with referring providers throughout the treatment process, including additional recommendations, referrals, and outcomes.
