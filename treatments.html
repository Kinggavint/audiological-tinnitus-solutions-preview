Treatments

A full menu of tinnitus treatments, matched to you.

Lenire is one of several evidence-based tools we use. The right tool depends on your tinnitus profile.

Lenire

FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation for chronic subjective tinnitus. 12-week in-home program with regular clinic visits.

Learn more

Levo System

An overnight, neuroscience-based tinnitus therapy that uses your tinnitus sound profile to deliver targeted sound therapy during sleep.

Neuromonics

A structured sound-therapy program designed to desensitize the brain to the tinnitus signal over several months.

Tinnitus Retraining Therapy

A counseling and sound-enrichment program based on the neurophysiological model of tinnitus, focused on changing your brain's response to the signal.

Combination Hearing Aids with Sound Therapy

Modern hearing aids with built-in tinnitus sound generators. Often the right starting point for patients who also have hearing loss.

Counseling and Sleep Strategy

Tinnitus often disrupts sleep and mood. We provide structured counseling and coordinate with other providers when indicated.

Hyperacusis Desensitization

For patients with reduced sound tolerance, we deliver a graded desensitization plan that returns comfort over weeks and months.

Care After Brain Injury

Tinnitus is common after concussion and TBI. As a CBIS, Dr. Cohen evaluates and treats these auditory effects within a coordinated rehab plan.

Not sure which treatment fits?

That is what the consultation is for. We assess first, then recommend.
