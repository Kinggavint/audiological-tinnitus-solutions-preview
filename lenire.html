Lenire

Lenire: FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation for tinnitus.

A designated Lenire treatment office serving Sherman Oaks and Southern California.

What is Lenire?

Lenire is the first FDA-cleared bimodal neuromodulation device for the treatment of chronic subjective tinnitus in adults. It combines gentle electrical stimulation of the tongue with personalized sound delivered through headphones. Together, these two signals retrain how the brain processes the tinnitus signal.

How the program works

  1. You complete an in-office tinnitus assessment to determine eligibility.
  2. We fit the device, calibrate the sound and stimulation settings, and teach you how to use it at home.
  3. You use Lenire for 60 minutes a day for 12 weeks.
  4. You return for scheduled follow-up visits so we can measure response and fine-tune the program.

Who is a candidate?

Lenire is appropriate for adults with chronic subjective tinnitus that has lasted longer than three months. Candidacy is determined by a clinical evaluation. Common reasons a patient may not be a candidate include certain medical implants, severe profound hearing loss, or active middle-ear disease.

What does the evidence show?

In the largest published clinical study of Lenire, the majority of treated patients reported clinically meaningful improvement in tinnitus severity after the 12-week course. Results are individual, and we set expectations honestly during your consultation. Lenire is one tool in a complete tinnitus program, not a magic switch.

Why choose a designated treatment office?

Lenire requires more than a device handoff. A designated treatment office has clinicians trained and certified by the manufacturer to deliver the program correctly. That training covers candidacy, calibration, troubleshooting, and follow-up.

Find out if Lenire is right for you.

The first step is a tinnitus consultation. We will review your history, evaluate your tinnitus, and tell you honestly whether Lenire is the right next step.
