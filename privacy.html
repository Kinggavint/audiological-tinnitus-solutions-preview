Privacy

Privacy Notice

We are committed to protecting the privacy of the individuals who visit our website and contact our clinic.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly through this website, including your name, phone, email, and the details you share in the appointment request form. We may also collect standard technical information such as browser type and pages viewed.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule appointments, coordinate care, and communicate about our services. We do not sell personal information.

Protected Health Information

Our clinic complies with applicable federal and state laws regarding the protection of health information, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). A full Notice of Privacy Practices is provided at the time of clinical care and is available upon request.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies and standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site and to improve content and functionality. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third Party Links

Our website may contain links to third party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The most current version will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice or how we handle information, please contact our office.
