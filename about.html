About

Tinnitus care, built around the patient.

Audiological Tinnitus Solutions is the dedicated tinnitus practice of Shahrzad Cohen, Au.D., CH-TM, CBIS.

A clinician certified in tinnitus management.

Dr. Shahrzad Cohen earned her Doctorate of Audiology and went on to complete the rigorous certification in tinnitus management (CH-TM). She is also a Certified Brain Injury Specialist (CBIS), a credential that matters because tinnitus often appears after concussion or head trauma.

Her Sherman Oaks practice has been recognized as a designated Lenire treatment office, and she is a 2025 Marquis Who's Who honored listee.

Audiological Tinnitus Solutions is the tinnitus-focused sister practice to Hearing Loss Solutions. Both share the same office, the same standards of care, and the same concierge model.

What makes our program different

  • Tinnitus is assessed in detail, not treated as an afterthought to a hearing test.
  • Treatment options span devices, therapy, and lifestyle support, not a single product line.
  • Coaching and follow-up are scheduled in advance, so progress is tracked over months.
  • We coordinate with physicians, mental health providers, and TBI rehabilitation teams when indicated.
  • Telehealth visits keep you supported between in-office appointments.
