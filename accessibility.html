Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We are committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience.

Our Commitment

We strive to make our website usable for individuals of all abilities. We continue to work toward compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level, and we welcome feedback that helps us improve.

Features That Support Accessibility

  • Semantic HTML structure with descriptive headings and landmarks.
  • Sufficient color contrast throughout the site.
  • Text at a minimum of 18 pixels for body content.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images.
  • Keyboard navigable menus, forms, and interactive components.
  • Descriptive link text and clear form labels.

In Person Accommodations

Our clinic aims to be accessible for patients with a wide range of hearing, communication, and physical needs. If you require an accommodation for your visit, please contact our office in advance so we can prepare appropriately.

Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you have suggestions for how we can improve accessibility, please contact our office.
